When an artist releases a powerful work and prepares a series of concerts, being present on the street makes all the difference. On this occasion, we have taken charge of the **poster pasting** to announce the arrival on stage of Grecas' latest album, titled *"Escrito en la M-30"*. With a clear **street marketing** strategy, we have brought the group's image and all the information about their tour into the view of thousands of pedestrians.

## A visual journey for the *"Tour 2026"*

The campaign seeks to connect directly with the urban audience that consumes live music. The poster design, focused on the visual universe of *"Escrito en la M-30"*, clearly highlights the confirmed stops of their *"Tour 2026"*, ensuring that fans identify at a glance when and where they will be able to see the group live.

Among the main dates and cities set out in the poster advertising are:

- 27/03 - Sala Razzmatazz 1, Barcelona.
- 17/04 - Roig Arena, Valencia.
- 17/05 - Sala La Riviera, Madrid.
- Concerts scheduled in other cities such as Murcia, Zaragoza, Alicante, Pamplona, Bilbao, A Coruña, Granada, Sevilla or Málaga.

By placing the information at strategic points of passage, we ensure that the tour announcement enters through people's eyes as they walk around the city.

## The value of physical poster advertising in music promotion

Although the digital environment is indispensable nowadays, traditional poster pasting still offers a real and hard-to-ignore impact. A well-placed poster generates conversation, reinforces the artist's image and awakens immediate interest in buying tickets.

For tours with an identity as defined as Grecas', outdoor presence works as a constant loudspeaker. By integrating the media into the daily urban environment, the message reaches music lovers, regular concertgoers and people looking for cultural plans in a natural and non-intrusive way.
