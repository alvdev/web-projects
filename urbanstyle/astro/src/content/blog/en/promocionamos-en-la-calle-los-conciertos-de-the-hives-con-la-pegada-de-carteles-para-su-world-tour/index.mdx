![We promote The Hives concerts on the street with poster pasting for their "World Tour 2025"](./header.jpg)

When a powerful rock band announces dates in our country, promotion has to get noticed from the very first moment. To announce the confirmed performances within their *"World Tour 2025"* tour, we carried out a **poster pasting** action on the street, ensuring that the group's announcement catches the attention of pedestrians during their daily routines.

Outdoor advertising remains an essential tool to connect directly with the public. On this occasion, the poster graphic—with the members wearing royal crowns and capes against a dark background—stands out very clearly against the concrete structures and urban environment.

## Direct visual impact at street level

The main objective of this **street marketing** campaign was to achieve immediate visibility coinciding with the ticket release. Placing the posters on structures located in pedestrian areas allows information to reach thousands of people naturally as they walk through the city.

The posters affixed to public roads announce the official dates scheduled in Spain:

- November 1: Sant Jordi Club in Barcelona.
- November 2: Movistar Arena in Madrid.

In addition, at the bottom of the graphic, official sales outlets such as livenation.es, Ticketmaster.es, El Corte Inglés.es and thehives.com are detailed, allowing anyone who sees the poster to know instantly where to buy their tickets.

## The effectiveness of paper in the urban environment

Combining a design with personality and proper placement on the street guarantees that the message does not go unnoticed. Unlike ads on screens or social media, physical presence on public roads accompanies citizens in their daily routines and reinforces event recall.
