The indie band Viva Suecia  have reactivated their activity on social media announcing the release of a new single titled “La Voz del Presidente”, now available on the different digital platforms.

Rafa Val, Alberto Cantúa, Jess Fabric and Fernando Campillo are the members of Viva Suecia. A group of friends who little by little and working very hard have managed to place themselves on their own merits among the  most important music bands on the national indie rock scene.

> Urban Style Publicity has collaborated with them on the campaign for the new release of their new single by carrying out a poster pasting in several cities in Spain ahead of the imminent start of their long and awaited tour that will take them to many cities in Spain eager to recover live music after the pandemic that deprived us of live shows. In cities like Madrid they have already hung the TICKETS SOLD OUT poster.
