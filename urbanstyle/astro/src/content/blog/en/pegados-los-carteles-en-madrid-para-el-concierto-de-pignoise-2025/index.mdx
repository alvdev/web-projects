Pignoise was founded in Madrid at the beginning of this century and brought together Álvaro Benito (player for Real Madrid, Getafe and the Spanish national team) and fellow footballer Héctor Polo. Pablo Alonso joined them, and they immediately began recording albums and giving concerts.

After «Melodías desafinadas» (2003) and «Esto no es un disco de punk» (2005), they recorded the song «Nada que perder» as the theme song for the Spanish television series «Los hombres de paco». It was a great success.

They are currently celebrating their 20th anniversary at the WiZink Center in Madrid on the upcoming 7 March 2025. And, as has become customary, we have already started pasting the concert promotion posters a year before the event.
