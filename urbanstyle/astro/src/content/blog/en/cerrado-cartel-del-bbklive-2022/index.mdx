[BBK Live 2022](https://www.bilbaobbklive.com/es) will be three days of festival in Kobetamendi where bands of all genres will gather and whose headliners will be LCD Soundsystem, The Killers, Placebo, Stromae, Pet Shop Boys or J Balvin among many more. A convincing and very danceable lineup.

> The dates will be July 7, 8 and 9, 2022 and you can buy your tickets here.
