Madrid is the city chosen by The Rolling Stones to begin their next European tour, «SIXTY» (sixty), with which they celebrate sixty years together. It will be next June 1 at the Wanda Metropolitano, as they have announced on Twitter.

<div align="center">
  ![Twitter screenshot of the Rolling
  Stones](Captura-de-pantalla-2022-03-22-a-las-12.23.35.png)
</div>

The band will tour ten countries with this tour and will offer two concerts in London. The last time Mick Jagger and company performed in Madrid was in 2014, at the Santiago Bernabéu stadium as part of «14 on Fire Tour».

According to promoter Live Nation, tickets will go on sale this Friday, March 18 at 10:00. They can be purchased on the websites doctormusic.com, entradas.com, livenation.es, ticketmaster.es and lasttour.org.

The group arrives at this tour with a notable absence, that of drummer Charlie Watts, who died last August at the age of 80 after more than half a century of career in their ranks.

For this anniversary, The Stones, who last year did a tour of the United States (No Filter), have prepared a new production and a new design of their famous tongue. You will be able to see that new design in the poster pasting in both Madrid and Barcelona announcing the tour of their Satanic Majesties.
