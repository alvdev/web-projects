![We conquered the center of Madrid with the poster pasting for "Walls en el Movistar Arena"](./header.jpg)

When an artist like Walls announces a concert at the Movistar Arena, we know we have to make a move. This time we prepared a **poster pasting** in a Madrid metro station so that nobody would miss the date. The result was an outdoor advertising deployment that turned the corridors into a preview of the show.

## A poster pasting that does not go unnoticed

The campaign focused on the metro environment, a space of constant passage. We placed the posters on the station walls, taking advantage of the flow of travelers. The objective? That every person passing through would see the name of Walls and the concert dates before getting home.

This is what makes **street marketing** special as a technique: it turns the daily commute into an encounter with music. Nobody needs to look for the ad; the ad finds the pedestrian.

## A show that promises to be unforgettable

Walls returns to the stage after the success of his first album, and does so with a show that mixes current pop with urban rhythms. The date is February 21, 2026 at the Movistar Arena in Madrid. Tickets are already on sale at Live Nation, Ticketmaster and El Corte Inglés, and the last ones remain.

Our poster pasting had to live up to that moment. Therefore, each poster was placed with precision, seeking maximum visibility in the station. It is not just about sticking paper; it is about creating a visual journey that accompanies the viewer until the day of the concert.

## Why street marketing works for concerts

Why do we bet on street marketing to launch a concert? The answer is simple: because it works. This type of outdoor advertising generates a direct impact on the public that moves around the city. In addition, poster pasting allows reaching a very varied audience, from the student going to class to the worker leaving the office.

These are some of the advantages we saw when choosing this campaign:

- 24/7 visibility in a high-traffic area.
- Adjusted cost compared to other advertising media.
- Direct impact on people moving around the city.
- An aesthetic finish that attracts attention without being invasive.

In short, the poster pasting for the Walls concert at the Movistar Arena was a safe bet. And we, delighted to have been part of such an exciting project.
