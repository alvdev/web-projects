![We bring Eros Ramazzotti's concert and his "Una historia Importante World Tour" to the streets of Madrid](./header.jpg)

The return of great artists to the stage always requires a clear and visible street-level promotion strategy. On this occasion, we worked on promoting Eros Ramazzotti's live show in Madrid, who will perform at the Movistar Arena on May 4, 2026 as part of his *"Una historia Importante World Tour"*.

## Street-level urban visibility

For an event of this scale to connect with people, presence in daily surroundings is fundamental. Through **wildposting** on strategically located urban displays and street furniture, we ensure the musical event is present in pedestrians' day-to-day lives. In busy areas, next to shops and high-foot-traffic thoroughfares, the concert image immediately catches the attention of anyone walking through the area.

## A clear campaign to inform fans

Promotional actions in urban spaces offer a direct impact that complements social media communication very well. The promotional poster displays key show information cleanly and directly so no one is left without a ticket:

- Artist: Eros Ramazzotti
- Tour: *"Una historia Importante World Tour"*
- Date: May 4, 2026
- Venue: Movistar Arena, Madrid

## Why opt for street actions

**Street marketing** allows concerts and cultural events to integrate naturally into the target audience's routine. Good **wildposting** generates conversation and reminds fans that tickets are already on sale.
