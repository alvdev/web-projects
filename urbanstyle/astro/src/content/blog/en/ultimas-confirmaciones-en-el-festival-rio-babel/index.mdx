The Festival Rio Babel 2022, which will be held from June 30 to July 2 at La Caja Mágica in Madrid, already has three headliners of the most varied kind and with unquestionable popular appeal.

* Thursday, June 30: Dani Martín
* Friday, July 1: C. Tangana
* Saturday, July 2: Residente

Season tickets can now be purchased on the festival's official website from 80 euros. In addition, if you want to see Dani Martín at Río Babel 2022, there is a quota of tickets on offer for 35 euros for that day. Residente's day costs the same, while C. Tangana's goes up to 40 euros.

After the success achieved in its adapted format, bringing together 40.000 attendees at the Wanda Metropolitano Stadium during a series of concerts last July, the Festival Río Babel will return in 2022 with its original format.

An awaited return, in which the Madrid festival changes location to La Caja Mágica. Thus growing in dimensions and capacity for this new edition, which is expected, this time around, to be the one of the reunion with the old normality.
