Outdoor advertising is very effective and is an excellent way to get a message to people, whether it is a promotion of a certain product, the announcement of a concert or sporting event, or any other kind of message.

Many studies have been conducted on the effectiveness of this way of reaching the target audience. One of them is the one carried out some time ago by the well-known American agency Ogilvy Action. This study reveals that classic advertising posters are much more effective than many other advertising platforms.

Posters placed at strategic points are a good marketing tool. These can notably improve impulse buying, one of the pillars of current commerce and with which large amounts of money can be raised.

It is estimated that around 29% of people make these purchases thanks to posters, which has positioned this veteran format as one of the most important resources for the marketing world.
