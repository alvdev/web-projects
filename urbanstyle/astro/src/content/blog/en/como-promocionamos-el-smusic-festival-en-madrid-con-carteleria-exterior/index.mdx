![How we promoted the "SMUSIC FESTIVAL" in Madrid with outdoor posters](./header.jpg)

Madrid is a city that never stops, and live music is part of its daily life. Very recently, we were in charge of giving street visibility to *"SMUSIC FESTIVAL"*, an event celebrating its first edition next Friday, June 27 at Recinto Iberdrola Music. To ensure the news reached thousands of people, we carried out a **flyposting** campaign designed to capture the attention of lovers of music in Spanish.

## A direct visual impact for *"SMUSIC FESTIVAL"* in Madrid

When promoting a music event of this magnitude, presence on public thoroughfares is a key pillar. Through **street marketing** actions, we got the artists' names to stand out on urban surfaces and high-footfall areas. The campaign combined a colorful and highly visual design with distribution on murals where large typography made it impossible not to notice.

On this occasion, the festival's artwork highlighted big names in music:
- Maná and Peso Pluma at the top of the musical lineup.
- Orishas, Cupido, and Delaporte bringing a variety of genres.
- Airbag and Amparanoia completing a lineup full of energy.

## Individual posters and QR codes to connect with the audience

For this action, we did not use a single artwork model. The wall was dressed with a grid-style composition combining individual posters dedicated to each artist (with custom designs for Cupido, Delaporte, Amparanoia, or Peso Pluma) alongside a general poster with all the event information.

In addition, each poster featured an integrated clear QR code with the message *"Get your ticket here!"*, which redirected directly to the smusicfestival.com website. In this way, traditional flyposting also works as a digital bridge, allowing people to buy their ticket at the very moment they see the advertisement while walking down the street.
