![How we took the "BTS World Tour" poster pasting to the streets of Madrid](./header.jpg)

Madrid already knows that BTS is returning to the stage. We have taken charge of taking to the streets to announce the dates of the *"BTS World Tour"* on 26 and 27 June at the Riyadh Air Metropolitano. If you want your event to resonate throughout the city, **poster pasting** continues to be one of the most direct and efficient ways of connecting with the public.

## A direct visual impact in public spaces

The K-pop phenomenon moves crowds and the expectation to see RM, Jin, Suga, J-Hope, Jimin, V and Jung Kook is enormous among the ARMY collective. For an event of this caliber, a **street marketing** action allows the information to reach pedestrians during their daily journey through the city.

The urban media used show all the key information that fans need:

- The confirmed dates for the performances in the capital.
- The venue of the event: the Riyadh Air Metropolitano stadium.
- The members of the group and the reference to register for the ticket pre-sale.

## Why urban advertising boosts your event

Being present in the urban environment makes a big difference when it comes to promoting large shows. A poster pasting executed rigorously helps to activate the conversation on social media, reinforce the artist's image and guarantee that the date stays in the fans' memory.

If you are organizing a concert, a festival or any cultural launch and you are looking for an urban campaign with a real presence on the street, we make sure your message does not go unnoticed.
