    ## Advertising with promoters
    It is a strategy where people (in many cases, women) are hired to promote products or services at events, fairs, exhibitions, etc. These promoters usually wear clothing of the brand or with distinctive elements of the advertising campaign.

    ## Event advertising
    It includes the promotion of specific events using various means, including the presence of people who act as event ambassadors, wear clothing with the event's brand, or distribute promotional materials.

    ## Merchandising
    This term refers to the practice of using branded products (such as clothing, accessories, posters, etc.) to promote a product or event. The presence of people using or distributing these products is part of the merchandising strategy.

    ## Guerrilla marketing
    An unconventional marketing technique that focuses on the use of surprising and unusual strategies, often involving direct public participation. This can include people dressed in a striking way to attract attention to a product or event.

    ## Brand activations
    Strategies designed to attract consumers' attention and generate direct interaction with the brand. Activations can include the presence of people dressed according to the campaign's theme and the distribution of promotional products.

    ## Street marketing
    A marketing strategy carried out in public places (such as streets, squares, etc.) that can include the presence of people dressed in a specific way to attract attention and promote a product or event.
