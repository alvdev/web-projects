<div class="container prose-2xl *:[h2]:font-bold *:[h2]:uppercase *:[h2]:leading-tight *:[h3]:font-bold">
  Wild posting is a trend within street marketing that acts as a
  visual communication medium, providing modern and prominent visibility in
  tune with new urban cultures. The process involves placing posters on
  large walls, each with different creatives, creating a composition with a
  global message. This message can consist of text, brand images, or
  visual elements. Generally, a color palette is used that conveys the
  brand's identity and evokes direct emotions in its target
  audience.
