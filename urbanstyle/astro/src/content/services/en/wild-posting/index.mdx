  <div>
    <h2 class="text-4xl font-extrabold uppercase text-black md:text-5xl">What is wild posting?</h2>
    <div class="mt-6 space-y-5 text-xl leading-relaxed text-gray-600">
      <p>Wild posting, also known as flyposting, is a <strong>street marketing</strong> technique that papers a wall, a construction hoarding or an authorized urban circuit with a series of posters. Each poster carries a different creative and, together, they build one single visual message. It is the difference between placing a poster and creating a wall that reads at a glance.</p>
      <p>It was born in the music scenes of New York and London, where labels announced concerts and records without the budget for large billboards. That urban, repetitive and direct aesthetic is now one of the most recognisable formats in outdoor advertising: it doesn't look like an ad, it looks like part of the city.</p>
      <p>At Urban Style Publicity we handle the whole process: we adapt or design the creatives as a series, print them, select the authorized walls and circuits, paste with our own teams and supervise the campaign from start to finish.</p>
    </div>
