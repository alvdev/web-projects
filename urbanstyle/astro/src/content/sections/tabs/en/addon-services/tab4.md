<h3 class="text-4xl font-bold">Photographic report</h3>

<ul>
  <li>The execution of the campaign is documented graphically upon completion of the poster pasting.</li>
  <li>It includes images of the locations where the posters have been pasted to verify their correct placement.</li>
  <li>The report provides information on the distribution of the materials and serves as proof of the work carried out.</li>
  <li>It is delivered to the client as part of the complete campaign service.</li>
</ul>
