<h3 class="text-4xl font-bold">Maintenance and replacement</h3>

<ul>
  <li>Periodic monitoring service for the condition of the posters during the duration of extensive campaigns.</li>
  <li>Includes inspections to identify deteriorated materials and their replacement when necessary.</li>
  <li>The frequency of inspections and the replacement criteria are adapted to the characteristics of each campaign.</li>
  <li>This service ensures that the posters maintain their appearance and effectiveness throughout the entire display period.</li>
</ul>
